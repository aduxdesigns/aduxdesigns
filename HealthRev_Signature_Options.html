Add your signature to Outlook
  1. Click Copy signature under the version you want.
  2. In Outlook, open the Signatures tool (File > Options > Mail > Signatures, or in new Outlook: Settings > Accounts > Signatures).
  3. Click New, name it, then click in the editing box and paste (Ctrl+V). Save.
